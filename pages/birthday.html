May your next chapter be filled with unexpected happiness, unforgettable memories, quiet peace, and moments that make you stop and think, 'I'm really glad I'm here.'

Keep shining, Swaraa. Your story is still becoming something beautiful.

With lots of friendship and warm wishes, always. ♡
